# MarketScope v5.9.67 — Save / Manage Withdrawal Summary

The **SAVE / MANAGE PORTFOLIO SIMULATIONS** workspace now repeats the active recurring-income results before the simulation is saved.

For **Yearly Withdrawal** simulations it shows the same five cards as the live simulator:

- Annual withdrawal
- Rebalanced remaining
- Not rebalanced remaining
- Rebalance difference
- Withdrawals funded — RB funded / targeted and NR funded / targeted

For **Monthly Withdrawal** simulations it shows:

- Monthly withdrawal
- Rebalanced remaining
- Not rebalanced remaining
- Rebalance difference
- Positive months — RB positive / modeled and NR positive / modeled

The same annual/monthly withdrawal summary is also shown beneath each applicable entry in **Saved Simulations**, so the income assumptions and outcome remain visible without reopening the PDF.

New saved annual simulations also persist explicit Rebalanced and Not-Rebalanced funded/targeted withdrawal counts. Existing saved simulations remain compatible because v5.9.67 can derive the counts from their saved schedules.

No return, withdrawal, rebalancing, monthly-history, ranking, or PDF calculation methodology changed in this release.
