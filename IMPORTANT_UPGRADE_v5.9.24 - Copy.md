# MarketScope v5.9.24

Comparison cards retain instrument logos and now mirror the Market Navigator Card View workflow: 2-year mini chart, complete return history, clickable return-period dollar-profit calculation, investment simulation, analyst targets/implied upside, rating and signal badges, News, ETF Holdings, and inline Full Details with live intraday and selectable historical-year charts.

The scheduled Nasdaq universe refresh now records analyst-rating transitions for retained stocks. `data/universe_metadata.json` includes `analyst_rating_change_count` and `analyst_rating_changes`, and Market Navigator displays transitions such as `AAPL: Hold → Buy` alongside stocks added/removed.
