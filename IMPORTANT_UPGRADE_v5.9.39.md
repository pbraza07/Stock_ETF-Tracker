# MarketScope v5.9.40 - PDF Rebalanced vs Not-Rebalanced Withdrawal Results

Portfolio Simulator PDFs now mirror the v5.9.38 annual-withdrawal comparison shown in the app.

When annual withdrawals are enabled, the PDF includes:

- A strategy-comparison page with annual withdrawal, rebalanced remaining balance, not-rebalanced remaining balance, and ending-balance difference.
- A year-by-year side-by-side table comparing both strategies.
- A detailed Rebalanced annual-withdrawal schedule.
- A detailed Not Rebalanced annual-withdrawal schedule.
- Total withdrawn, remaining balance, and remaining + withdrawals for each strategy.

Older saved simulations are forced to rebuild into this PDF layout when opened, provided their saved record contains the v5.9.38 strategy schedules.
