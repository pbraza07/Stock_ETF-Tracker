# MarketScope v5.9.81 — All Monthly Cohort Depletion Dates

The monthly rolling Start-Year depletion dashboard now lists every modeled cohort inside both the Rebalanced and Not-Rebalanced cards.

Each cohort shows:

- the exact month the investment path started (`YYYY-MM`);
- the first month the account depleted (`YYYY-MM`); or
- `Not depleted through YYYY-MM` when the cohort survives the modeled window.

The original earliest-depletion month remains the large headline value. The cohort detail stays inside the bordered card, uses a compact two-column layout on wide screens, and automatically stacks for smaller screens.
