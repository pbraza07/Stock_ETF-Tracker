# MarketScope v5.9.82 — All Annual Cohort Depletion Years

The annual rolling Start-Year depletion dashboard now mirrors the detailed monthly cards.

Each Rebalanced and Not-Rebalanced box includes every modeled cohort with:

- the year the investment initiated;
- the first year the account depleted; or
- `Not depleted through YYYY` when the cohort survives the modeled window.

The previous caption text below the bordered cards has been removed. The earliest-depletion summary and all cohort details now remain inside each card.
