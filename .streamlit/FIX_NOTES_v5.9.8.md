# MarketScope v5.9.8 - Unlimited Stock Comparison

## Added
- Unlimited stock comparison workspace.
- Compare button on every stock card.
- Multi-row selection in Table View with an Add to Comparison action.
- Direct unlimited stock multiselect plus Add Filtered, Select All, and Clear actions.
- Comparison Cards with 12-card pagination and remove controls.
- Full Comparison Table with explicit sorting plus native header sorting.

## Comparison fields
- Symbol, company, sector, industry, price, market cap.
- Nasdaq analyst rating and Yahoo analyst target low/average/high with implied average move.
- Short, long, and fundamental buy signals.
- 1D, 1M, 3M, 6M, YTD, and every current 10 calendar-year return.
- Current MarketScope investment-simulation period, amount, ending value, profit/loss, and simulation return.

ETF Holdings behavior and the 213-ETF universe are unchanged.
