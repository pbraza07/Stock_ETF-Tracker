# MarketScope v5.9.41

## Market Table — Worst Year

Market Navigator > Table View now includes a **Worst Year** column for every instrument.

The value is derived from the instrument's actual available completed calendar-year return history (up to the latest 20 completed years). Missing years before a stock IPO or ETF inception are ignored rather than treated as 0%.

Example:

`2022 (-28.41%)`

All v5.9.40 persistence protections for saved Portfolio Simulation PDFs remain unchanged.
