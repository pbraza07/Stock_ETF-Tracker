# IMPORTANT UPGRADE — MarketScope v5.9.35

Sector Performance drill-down table semantics were corrected. The table now shows short-period returns plus the latest 20 completed calendar years as individual annual returns, not compounded 1Y–20Y horizon values. The Total Profit timeframe selector remains unchanged and continues to calculate cumulative/compounded performance for 1Y–20Y. Explicit dataframe column ordering prevents 12Y or other labels from appearing out of numerical order in supported horizon controls.
