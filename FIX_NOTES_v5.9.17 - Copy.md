# MarketScope v5.9.17 — 20 Years of Annual Returns

- Expanded completed calendar-year return history from 10 years to 20 years.
- During 2026 the annual return tiles/columns now cover 2025 through 2006 when an instrument has sufficient history.
- All 20 annual-return tiles remain clickable in Card View and calculate exact-period ending value and profit from the selected investment amount.
- Extended the historical investment simulator and portfolio split simulator from 1Y–10Y to 1Y–20Y.
- Extended the year-by-year chart selector from the current year + prior 10 years to the current year + prior 20 years.
- The scheduled/manual snapshot refresh continues to use MAX adjusted Yahoo history so older annual returns can be populated without fabricating missing pre-inception data.
- 10Y CAGR analytics remain 10-year metrics; this change adds annual return visibility and 20-year compounding choices rather than redefining 10Y CAGR.
